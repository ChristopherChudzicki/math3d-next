## Short version

- **99.91% of scenes (99.96% weighted) are pass-1-clean; 99.40% (99.79% weighted) are clean and fully checked.** The gap is `needs_eval`: cameras over a zero or symbolic axis scale and vector fields with symbolic samples, which need pass 2.
- **No scene crashes.**
- **One translator bug remains:** a negative axis scale falls back to the default, so the camera is mirrored (84 scenes, weight 614). Both apps multiply by the signed scale.
- **Four owner policies change what math3d-react showed** (below). One, blank calculated visibility, hides items in 12 of the 100 most-viewed scenes; those need a check by hand before the cutover ([README](../README.md#manual-checks)).
- **The audit checks itself:** five bugs injected into the translator are each flagged (mutation test), and four independent review agents re-checked every policy call and claim against both apps' code and the data.

## Owner policies

Each one deliberately differs from what math3d-react rendered:

- **`zOrder`:** math3d-next's automatic order (tree order, surfaces last; #1010) instead of math3d-react's `(1-opacity)·100`. Matters only where a translucent graphic overlaps another: 24% of scenes (29% weighted) have two or more translucent graphics.
- **Blank calculated visibility keeps the author's `visible`:** 12,285 items in 5,059 scenes (9% weighted), mostly the built-in `yz`/`zx` grids. math3d-react showed them on load, because toggling any switch variable turns calculated visibility on for every graphic; math3d-next hides them, as the author saw while editing. A blank expression, including LaTeX spacing, becomes empty.
- **Slider speeds 1.5× and 3× become 2× and 4×**, the nearest math3d-next options: 1,535 sliders in 1,364 scenes (1.6% weighted) animate a third faster.
- **Computed cameras don't update on drag.** math3d-react locked them; math3d-next would otherwise write numbers over their expressions after a click.

Also settled, matching math3d-react: slider `fps` 60 (its effective rate), polar r-range parameter `θ`.

## Translator bugs fixed

- Camera divided by the default axis scales (38,450 scenes), and orthographic cameras kept math3d-react's 50× dolly.
- Default items and folders merged whole, not per property: grid `axes` and `visible` (11,833 scenes), folder descriptions (26,647, editor only).
- Grid `width`/`snap` hard-coded (3,071 and 964 scenes).
- Unsupported slider speeds became `1` (1,309 scenes).
- Vector-field `samples` regex: kept `\right` (783 scenes), and split inside nested commas (76).
- Surface samples/grid counts not swapped when a range depends on the other parameter (26 scenes).
- Unknown legacy properties crashed the translator (1 scene).

## Remaining

- Negative axis scales (84 scenes): see the short version.
- `VARIABLE` names saved with a trailing `=` (2 scenes).
- `needs_eval`, for pass 2: 489 cameras over a zero or symbolic axis scale (or a non-numeric position), and 43 vector fields with symbolic samples.

## Rendering differences outside pass 1

- A vector field with 1 sample in a direction renders at the range minimum; math3d-react centred it. Filed as a math3d-next bug.
- A translucent surface drawn before an opaque one hides the opaque one's far side. Filed; pass 3 oversamples these scenes.
- A calculated visibility that fails to evaluate shows the item in math3d-react and hides it in math3d-next. Pass 2 counts these.
