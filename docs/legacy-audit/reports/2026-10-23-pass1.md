# Legacy audit, pass 1 — 2026-10-23

Method: [pass-1 spec](../2026-10-23-pass1-spec.md). Snapshot: prod, pulled 2026-10-22, 101,142 legacy scenes. math3d-next `legacy-audit/pass1`; math3d-react `master` (`2fce4dd`) plus the audit commit. The deployed math3d-react commit is not confirmed (the repo has no release tags). Aggregates only.

## Short version

- **Translation is right far more often than the 0% headline suggests.** One app-level choice, `zOrder`, makes every scene "unclean". Once three policy questions are settled (below), **57% of scenes (61% weighted) are pass-1-clean as of today**.
- **Two translator bugs account for most of the rest.** Fixing them lifts that to **93% of scenes (95% weighted)**. Fixing the next six small ones reaches **99%**.
  - **Camera ignores axis scales** (38,450 scenes): `get_axis_scales` reads `item["properties"]["scale"]`, but legacy items are flat, so every scene is divided by the default scales (1, 1, ½).
  - **Default items are merged whole instead of property by property** (11,833 scenes): a customized `grid-yz`/`grid-zx` loses its `axes` and renders as an xy grid. The same bug resets 26,647 default-folder descriptions to "Folder" (editor-only).
- **Only one scene crashes** (an unknown `samples` key on an explicit surface; a 500 in math3d-next). That one scene is why the local batch migration stopped at 97.5%.
- **The audit checks itself:** five bugs injected into the translator are each flagged (mutation test). The calibration set's camera and grid verdicts were confirmed by hand against the raw data.

## Findings

### Translator bugs (filed under the epic)

- **Camera position/target divided by default axis scales**: 38,450 scenes, render. The z-scale case alone doubles the camera's z distance.
- **Orthographic camera keeps math3d-react's 50× dolly**: about 6,900 of those scenes. math3d-next applies its own dolly. Likely a bug; confirm in pass 3.
- **`default_items`/`DEFAULT_FOLDERS` merged at item level**: grid `axes` (11,833 scenes) and default `visible: false` (1,676) lost; folder descriptions (26,647, editor).
- **Grid `width` and `snap` hard-coded**: 3,071 and 964 scenes.
- **Slider `speedMultiplier` outside the translator's list becomes `1`**: 1,309 scenes.
- **Vector-field `samples` regex**: the raw string's `\r` in `\right` matches a carriage return, so `samples3` keeps `\right`. 783 scenes.
- **Unknown legacy keys crash `translate_*`**: 1 scene, a 500 today.

### Policy questions (need an owner decision, not a translator fix)

- **`zOrder`**: math3d-react orders transparent items by opacity, `(1-opacity)·100`. The translator writes `""`, which tells math3d-next to use its own order, surfaces last. It affects nearly every scene, but only where translucent items overlap.
- **Calculated visibility**: 3,381 items have `useCalculatedVisibility` on, a blank expression and `visible: false`. math3d-react shows them on load: the `visible` handler is skipped, and toggling any switch turns `useCalculatedVisibility` on for all graphics. The translator hides them, as their authors set.
- **Minor, recommend accepting**:
  - slider `fps` 30 vs math3d-react's effective 60 (smoothness only; speed is unchanged);
  - computed cameras with `updateOnDrag: true` (1,650 scenes);
  - polar range parameter `Q` vs `θ`, which is math3d-next's own convention (none of the 3,470 affected ranges mention θ);
  - surface sample/grid order when a range depends on the other parameter (under 100 scenes; check in pass 3).

## Deferred

- `needs_eval` (4,577 comparisons; mostly symbolic axis scales and 1.5×/3× slider speeds) goes to pass 2/3.
- Pass-3 oversampling flags: custom axis scale (39,922 scenes), orthographic camera (7,208), computed camera (1,650).

## After translator fixes (branch `legacy-audit/translator-fixes`)

Owner decisions applied to the table:

- `zOrder` takes math3d-next's tree order (#1010);
- slider `fps` takes math3d-next's 50;
- a blank calculated visibility keeps the author's `visible`;
- computed cameras keep math3d-next's default `updateOnDrag` (math3d-react disables dragging them, so it has no effect).

Translator fixed:

- cameras use the real axis scales and are un-dollied when orthographic;
- per-property defaults;
- grid `width`/`snap`;
- slider speeds;
- vector-field samples;
- unknown properties and unusable axis scales.

The polar r-range parameter is now `θ`, in both the translator and math3d-next's default config. `Q` there was a stray, not a convention.
