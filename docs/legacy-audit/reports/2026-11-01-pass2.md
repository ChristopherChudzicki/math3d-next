## Short version

- **94.6% of weight is pass-2-clean** (94.2% of scenes), against the 95% target.
- A complex output of a graphic's expression becomes `NaN` in math3d-next (`realFunc`) and isn't drawn. That's the intended behaviour (owner decision 2026-11-01), so those `complex_vs_nan` pairs are allowlisted. Without the allowlist, 88.0% of weight is clean. A complex surface domain is not covered.
- The largest causes left are missing builtins (weight 31.5k), `pdiff` (17.4k), `|` as `dot` (14.9k) and `index()` read 0-based (14.7k).
- Two causes are new since the spec: 2-argument `arctan` takes its arguments in the other order (fixed by #1424 after this run), and an assignment inside a call (`f(t=0)`) leaks into the scope.
