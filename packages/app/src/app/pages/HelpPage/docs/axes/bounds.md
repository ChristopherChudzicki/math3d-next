---
name: axis bounds
latex: x_{\operatorname{min}}
keyboard: x_min
summary: Bounds of each axis's range
tags:
  - axes
---

- \(x_{\operatorname{min}}\), \(x_{\operatorname{max}}\), \(y_{\operatorname{min}}\), \(y_{\operatorname{max}}\), \(z_{\operatorname{min}}\) and \(z_{\operatorname{max}}\) are the bounds set on each axis.
- For example, a slider's range could be \(\left[x_{\operatorname{min}}, x_{\operatorname{max}}\right]\).

If you define your own `x_min`, yours is used instead.
