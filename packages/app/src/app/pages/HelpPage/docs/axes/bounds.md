---
name: axis bounds
latex: x_{\operatorname{min}}
keyboard: x_min
summary: The x-axis's lower bound
tags:
  - axes
---

- \(x*{\operatorname{min}}\), \(x*{\operatorname{max}}\), \(y*{\operatorname{min}}\), \(y*{\operatorname{max}}\), \(z*{\operatorname{min}}\) and \(z*{\operatorname{max}}\) are the bounds set on each axis.
- For example, a slider's range could be \(\left[x_{\operatorname{min}}, x_{\operatorname{max}}\right]\).

If you define your own `x_min`, yours is used instead.
